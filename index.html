Scientific honesty

What FarmShift does — and does not — claim.

FarmShift is a comparative decision-support prototype. NASA environmental observations provide context, while the rotation model uses simplified crop characteristics and farm inputs. The result is an explainable comparison, not a guaranteed yield prediction or a substitute for local agronomic advice.
